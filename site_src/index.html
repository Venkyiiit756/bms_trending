    About the data
    • The figure is BookMyShow's own "tickets booked in the last 1 hour" for each movie. It is a rolling count, so readings 30 minutes apart overlap; they are not 30-minute sales.
    • Smaller films, and most films late at night, show "last 24 hours" instead; those are listed separately and never mixed with hourly figures.
    • The count is per BookMyShow listing, across all its languages and formats, and is not city-specific. "Jailer 2" covers Tamil, Telugu and Kannada; "Jailer 2 (Hindi)" is listed separately.
    • Each snapshot uses the latest reading at or before that time; the reading time is shown. Figures are rounded by BookMyShow (e.g. 14.92K) and are tickets, not revenue.
    • Unofficial: collected from the BookMyShow app's public data. Not affiliated with BookMyShow.
