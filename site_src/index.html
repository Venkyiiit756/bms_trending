Each figure is BookMyShow's rolling count, so the 30-minute snapshots overlap and show the trend more often (they are not 30-minute sales). Each value is the movie's latest reading at or before the snapshot time (up to 75 minutes old; smaller films are checked hourly, so their reading time can be earlier). A movie shows in the "last 1 hour" table only while BookMyShow shows that figure for it; at night and for small films it shows the 24-hour figure instead. Counts are tickets booked, not revenue. The counter is per movie listing (all its languages and formats, for example Jailer 2 = Tamil, Telugu and Kannada; the Hindi version is listed separately) and is not city-specific. Stale cached readings are filtered out.
