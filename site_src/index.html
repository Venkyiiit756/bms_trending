How these totals are worked out
  • A day here is a real 24 hours counted from the "From" time, so a day can run from noon to noon.
  • BookMyShow shows its own "booked in the last 24 hours" figure late at night for big films (about 2 to 7 AM) and all day for smaller films. When a day ends at such a time (or within an hour of one, corrected by the estimate for the minutes in between), that figure is used (marked "BookMyShow").
  • Otherwise the day is estimated from the hourly figures taken every 30 minutes. Hourly figures added up come out about 15 to 20% below BookMyShow's own 24-hour figure, so each film's daytime figures are scaled by how the two compared on recent nights (the factor is shown, about ×1.2). Night hours, when no hourly figure shows, are a small allowance. Estimates are marked ≈.
  • Checked against 45 nights of BookMyShow 24-hour figures (each night left out of the scaling it was checked with): typical error under 1%, nine in ten within 4%. Films that rarely show an hourly figure get no estimate, only BookMyShow's own figures.
  • Pick "Days from 4 AM" to make every full day an exact BookMyShow figure.
  • The count is per BookMyShow listing across all its languages and is not city-specific.

    About the data
    • The figure is BookMyShow's own "tickets booked in the last 1 hour" for each movie. It is a rolling count, so readings 30 minutes apart overlap; they are not 30-minute sales.
    • Smaller films, and most films late at night, show "last 24 hours" instead; those are listed separately and never mixed with hourly figures.
    • The count is per BookMyShow listing, across all its languages and formats, and is not city-specific. "Jailer 2" covers Tamil, Telugu and Kannada; "Jailer 2 (Hindi)" is listed separately.
    • Each snapshot uses the latest reading at or before that time; the reading time is shown. Figures are rounded by BookMyShow (e.g. 14.92K) and are tickets, not revenue.
    • Unofficial: collected from the BookMyShow app's public data. Not affiliated with BookMyShow.
